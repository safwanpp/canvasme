Malayalam fonts bundled with canvas.me

All fonts here are free software under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(some SMC fonts are dual-licensed OFL / GPLv3 with font exception).
Each family's copyright notice and license text is in licenses/.

Swathanthra Malayalam Computing (https://smc.org.in, https://gitlab.com/smc/fonts):
  Rachana, Meera, Karumbi, Dyuthi, Anjali Old Lipi, Malini, Nupuram
  (Regular, Condensed, Semi Condensed, Semi Expanded, Expanded, Sharp, SuperSoft,
  Display, Calligraphy, Dots, Color)
  Keraleeyam, Uroob, Suruma, Raghu Malayalam Sans: TrueType builds from the Debian
  fonts-smc-* packages; their Debian copyright files are in licenses/.
Rachana Institute of Typography (https://gitlab.com/rit-fonts/): licenses/rit-fonts.txt
Lohit Malayalam (Red Hat, https://pagure.io/lohit): licenses/lohit-malayalam.txt

TrueType sources were converted to WOFF2 without changing any glyphs.
